# Mood Moves

A private behavioural activation tracker for the Inner Anchor forest walk group.

- Works in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) on phones and computers.
- No accounts, no sign-up, no server, no AI.
- Every entry is saved only in the browser of the device that made it. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the facilitator cannot see it.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole app |
| `manifest.webmanifest` | Lets people "Add to Home Screen" so it opens like an app |
| `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | Home-screen icons |

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free, about 10 minutes, no coding)

1. Sign in at github.com (or create a free account).
2. Click **+** (top right) → **New repository**. Name it `mood-moves`, choose **Public**, and click **Create repository**.
3. On the new repository page, click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in all five files above (not the folder itself), then click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, **Branch** to `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
5. Wait a minute or two and refresh. The page will show your link, like:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/mood-moves/`

That link is what you share with participants. The repository holds only the app code, never anyone's entries.

To update the app later, upload the new `index.html` to the same repository (it replaces the old one). Participants' saved entries are kept, because they live on their phones and not in the code.

## Tell participants

- Open the link in your normal browser, not a private/incognito window.
- **Add it to your home screen**:
  - iPhone (Safari): Share button → *Add to Home Screen*.
  - Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → *Add to Home screen* or *Install app*.
- This matters most on iPhone. Safari can clear a website's saved data after about a week without visits, but apps added to the home screen are exempt.
- Clearing browser data deletes your entries. Use **My data → Copy backup** now and then and paste it into a note.
- Each device keeps its own entries separately. To move to a new phone, copy your backup on the old one and use **Restore** on the new one.
